# Monthly Activity UI Inspiration

## Output

Write the findings to `docs/monthly-activity-ui-inspiration.md`.

## Research

Investigate commercially successful, widely downloaded, or otherwise proven focus, habit, and activity-tracking apps with polished daily and monthly history experiences. Determine which interaction patterns should inform the planned Compact Monthly Activity refinement in 1000 Pomodoros.

## Include

- Five to seven relevant case studies selected for both product success and functional similarity.
- A defensible success signal for each case study, such as an official download band, public user count, subscriber count, or reported revenue; distinguish company-wide scale from feature-specific adoption and label third-party estimates.
- Evidence-backed observations of how each product presents summary metrics, recent activity, month navigation, calendars or heatmaps, daily detail, progressive disclosure, and empty or dense states.
- Direct links to the strongest sources and the date or period attached to scale claims.
- A comparison of calendar-first, ledger-first, and hybrid approaches for a 320px responsive web surface with 44px targets and a 200%-zoom-equivalent viewport.
- Transferable visual and interaction principles that fit the product's simple, minimal, tomato-led design language.
- A concrete verdict on the current proposal: three newest active days on Home, seven on Journey Detail, newest first, month summary before detail, seven-day reveal batches, and reversible collapse.
- Any justified adjustment to `context/current-feature.md`, clearly separated from future ideas that should remain out of scope.

## Sources

- Current official App Store and Google Play listings, official product/help pages, company press or investor-relations material, and public filings.
- Reputable third-party reporting only when a primary source is unavailable; do not present estimated revenue as reported revenue.
- Existing repository product, design, feature, and Monthly Activity implementation context.
- Current rendered app behavior and screenshots where useful, without mutating persisted user data.
